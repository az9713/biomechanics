The margin b is the distance still left from ξ to the front edge of the base of support - the amount of extra lean or impulse a fixed-support (ankle/hip) response can still arrest before a step becomes mandatory. A forward impulse J moves ξ toward the edge; when it would carry ξ past it, b is spent.

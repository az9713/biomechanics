## Geometry-driven heroes (not third-party art)

| Hero | Landmarks |
|------|-----------|
| `femur_lateral` | Head, neck-shaft ≈125°, greater trochanter, shaft, condyles |
| `hip_joint` | Ilium fragment, acetabular arc, femoral head + neck |
| `knee` | Femoral condyles, tibial plateau, patella, contact line |
| `shoulder` | Scapula, shallow glenoid, humeral head, clavicle stub |
| `lumbar` | Stacked bodies + discs |
| `foot_lateral` | Calcaneus, medial arch, toes |

These are **teaching reconstructions**, not freehand cartoons and not claimed as traced licensed plates.
